How expensive is an effect?

Filters are usually fine when they are small and still. The cost grows when the browser has to blur a large area, especially when that area changes on every frame.

How I would check the cost

  1. Turn on Show paint flashing from the DevTools command palette, then scroll the page.
  2. Record a few seconds in the Performance panel while scrolling, hovering, and animating.
  3. Look for frames over 16ms. Paint points to repaints; Composite Layers points to layer pressure.
